WashPadi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

WashPadi is an app for laundry and dry-cleaning shops to record orders, customers, payments and expenses. It is published by Sodiq Yusuff ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Who uses WashPadi

WashPadi is used by shop owners. Shop owners enter information about their own customers. For that customer information, the shop owner decides what is recorded, and we store it on their behalf.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and name, and your sign-in method (email and password, Google or Apple).
  • Shop details: business name, phone, email, address, CAC number, logo, and the bank account details you choose to print on receipts.
  • Records you enter: customers (name, phone number, address, notes), orders and items, prices, payments, customer wallet balances, old debts and expenses.
  • Contacts (optional): if you allow it, you can pick a contact to add as a customer. Only the contacts you pick are saved. We never upload your whole address book.
  • Photos (optional): only the image you choose as your shop logo.
  • Usage data: anonymous events about how the app is used (for example "order created" with item counts and amounts, app version, phone platform and OS version, and your shop name). These events never include customer names, phone numbers, addresses or notes.
  • Device info: platform (Android or iOS), app version and an app-generated device ID.

We do not collect your location, we do not show ads, and we do not sell data.

How we use it

  • To run the app: orders, receipts, reminders and reports.
  • To back up your shop to the cloud, so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app, using the usage data.
  • To contact you about your account if we need to.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your phone. When you sign in, it is also backed up to our cloud provider, Supabase, over an encrypted connection. Each shop can only reach its own data. Backup files you save or share yourself go wherever you send them.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and Expo (app updates). When you share a receipt or open WhatsApp from the app, the message goes through the app you choose. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your cloud data within 30 days. See Delete your account.

Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app at any time, export a backup file, and ask us to correct or delete your data. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you can also object to processing or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. A shop's customers who want their details changed or removed should contact that shop, or contact us.

Children

WashPadi is a business app and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.

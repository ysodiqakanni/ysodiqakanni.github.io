Delete your WashPadi account

WashPadi, published by Sodiq Yusuff

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send an email to sodiqayusuff@gmail.com with the subject Delete my WashPadi account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to WashPadi, and include your shop name.
  3. We will reply to confirm the request, then delete your data within 30 days.

If you want a copy first, go to More → Backup in the app and save a backup file before you ask.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account (email and name).
  • Your shop's cloud backup: shop details, logo, bank details, customers, orders, payments, wallets, debts, expenses and price list.
  • Usage events linked to your shop.

What we keep

  • Nothing that identifies you or your customers. We may keep a record that a deletion was done, and anything the law requires us to keep, for no longer than the law requires.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall WashPadi or clear its storage. Backup files you saved or shared yourself are not affected.

Deleting only some data

You can delete or edit customers, orders and expenses in the app. To remove only your cloud backup or a specific customer's details, email us at the same address.

Common questions

Does WashPadi work without internet?

Yes. You only need internet to sign in. After that you can take orders, record payments and share receipts offline. Changes are saved to your account the next time you're online.

I got a new phone. How do I get my shop back?

Install WashPadi, sign in with the same account, and tap Restore my shop. Your orders, customers, payments and logo come back. Use only one phone for your shop at a time.

How do I check that my shop is saved?

Open the More tab. It says "Saved to your account" when everything is backed up, or shows how many changes are still waiting.

Can I keep my own backup?

On Android, go to More → Backup to save a backup file to your phone or share a copy.

How do I send a receipt or a reminder?

Open the order. Tap View and share receipt to send the receipt, or Remind to send a payment reminder on WhatsApp. When an order is ready, you can tell the customer it's ready for pickup the same way.

A customer paid in advance or paid too much. What do I do?

Keep the money in the customer's wallet: open the customer and tap Add money, or approve putting the extra in their wallet when you record the payment. It's used on their next orders.

I made a mistake on an order.

You can edit an order's items, prices, discounts and pickup date any time before the customer collects it. Open the order and tap Edit next to Items.
